Privacy Policy

Written plainly, on purpose - this describes what actually happens in the code, not boilerplate.

Who runs this

Overview (Life Dashboard) is a personal project run by one person (Tyler Slep). There's no company, no data broker relationship, and nothing here is sold to anyone.

What's collected

  • Account info: username, email (optional but needed for password reset), and your password - stored as a one-way scrypt hash, never in plain text or anywhere reversible.
  • Whatever you choose to enter: financial figures (bank balances, card balances, transactions, subscriptions), to-dos, exam/countdown dates, calendar feed URLs, uploaded photos, notes, custom theme colors, and a saved weather location.
  • Nothing tracked automatically: no analytics, no ad pixels, no third-party trackers.

How data is protected

  • All traffic to this app is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).
  • Passwords are hashed with scrypt - not encrypted, not reversible, not visible to anyone including the admin.
  • Transaction details, card labels, and investment history are encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM) before being stored - a raw copy of the database wouldn't reveal them.
  • Account balance totals (e.g. "bank balance: $X") are stored as plain numbers, not encrypted - the app needs to sum and sort these directly in the database, and encrypting them would mean much more app code doing that math instead, which is a bigger correctness risk for a personal finance tool used daily. This is a deliberate tradeoff, not an oversight.
  • The database itself runs on Railway's managed Postgres, which encrypts its underlying storage at rest.

Who can see what

Every account's data is isolated by account - no one can read another account's financial data, photos, or notes through the app. The Robinhood/investment widgets specifically are tied to one admin account only; other accounts never see that data, by design, not just by convention.

Third parties

The only data that ever leaves this app for a third party: if you set a Weather widget location, your city name/coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo (a free weather API) to fetch a forecast. If you set an email address, it's used to send you account emails (like password resets) through a transactional email provider - nothing else is shared with them.

Cookies

One HttpOnly session cookie, used only to keep you signed in. Not used for tracking or advertising.

Deleting your data

You can permanently delete your account and everything tied to it at any time from Settings. This is immediate and can't be undone.
